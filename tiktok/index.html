What should you charge for a sponsored TikTok?

Tier rates, the per-view basis behind them, and why TikTok sits below Instagram.

Figures gathered 24 July 2026 from published benchmark data, cross-checked against deals creators and brand-side buyers report publicly. Full method and sources.

Rates by follower tier

Account sizePer video

Reported real deals cluster within this: a creator with 60,000 followers described averaging $1,000 to $2,000 a video, dropping to $500 plus commission for smaller brands they liked. An influencer manager said $500 secures a 25,000 to 30,000 follower creator, or 30,000 to 50,000 with negotiation, provided their reach and view counts are strong.

That proviso is the whole game. Views, not followers.

Why TikTok sits below Instagram

TikTok rates run roughly 10 to 25% under Instagram at nano and micro size, converging as accounts get larger. The baseline CPM is about $22 per thousand views against $27 for an Instagram Reel.

The gap is narrower than it used to be and narrower than some guides claim. One source put it at 40 to 60% lower; the more corroborated figure is the narrower one.

Engagement runs much higher here

TikTok averages about 4.25% engagement against Instagram's 0.98% by follower count. That means 4% engagement is strong on Instagram and slightly below par on TikTok, and quoting the same "high engagement" line on both platforms will read as naive to a buyer who knows the benchmarks.

By tier: nano accounts typically run 6.5%, micro around 4.5%, and macro accounts about 3%.

Niche still moves it more than size

The same multipliers apply as everywhere else. Finance around 2.25x a lifestyle audience, tech reviews 1.75x, beauty and fashion 1.35x, gaming level with lifestyle. A 30,000-follower finance account can be worth more than a 100,000-follower lifestyle one.
