What should you charge for a UGC video?

Priced on production and experience, not audience. That changes the maths entirely.

Figures gathered 24 July 2026 from published benchmark data, cross-checked against deals creators and brand-side buyers report publicly. Full method and sources.

Rates by experience

The market median is about $175 a video.

Experience levelPer video
Beginner$75–300
Mid-tier$300–1,000
Experienced$600–3,000+

The median across the market is around $175 and the average sits between $150 and $212. If your rate is well below that and you are producing usable footage, you are underpricing.

Follower count barely matters

This is what separates UGC from every other kind of brand deal. The brand posts the content, not you, so they are buying production rather than reach. A creator with 2,000 followers who shoots well is worth more than one with 200,000 who does not.

Rate your work on how good the footage is, how fast you turn it round, and how little the brand has to fix. Not on your audience.

What raises the number

The add-ons that apply here.

Add-onTypical uplift

Usage rights matter more in UGC than anywhere else, because the entire point of the content is that the brand runs it. A rate quoted without asking about usage duration is a rate quoted blind.

Watch for the lowball floor

Creators repeatedly report cold inbound offers landing at $150 to $200 regardless of what they are worth, including one with 9 to 12% engagement being offered that for multiple videos. Brands fishing at that level rarely move far.

If your number and their number are a long way apart, that is usually information about who you are talking to rather than about your pricing.
